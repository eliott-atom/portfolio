I am Eliott, I live in Geneva and studied
in EPFL in Lausanne and TU in Berlin

Since forever, I have spent my time in theaters and doing my own films ; writing, acting, and building the spaces where stories happen. I was lucky enough to get funding for a play in Nyon. A film I made recently received the Matura Arbeit Preis and I continue to make others.

I see architecture as a continuation of this work. It is the study of how an architectured work determines a movement or a feeling. I am looking for a way to build that remains sensible, accessible to everyone and adapted to the state of the planet.
